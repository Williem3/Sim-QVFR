# Assetto Corsa Competizione

## Must-knows

- **One-time setup first:** ACC's VR has to run through OpenComposite. Follow
  [ACC in VR through OpenComposite](../acc-opencomposite.md) (about five minutes: one file swapped in ACC's folder,
  easy to undo). The launcher's **Launch checks** tells you if it's missing.
- **Version 1.10.4** (Steam). On any other build, Sim-QVFR stays off and ACC runs as usual.
- **Then pick Assetto Corsa Competizione** in the launcher's game list and click **Launch**. The launcher starts ACC
  in VR mode for you.
- **Eye tracking works** without any other tool.
- **On Pimax and Varjo,** Sim-QVFR puts the picture together itself in ACC (the **Let the runtime compose** option is
  Le Mans Ultimate only).
- **Online play** is at your own risk: see [Online play and anti-cheat](online-play.md).

What works in ACC: the sharp area that follows your eyes, the headset menu, the performance overlay, the view crop,
the helmet overlay, resolution changes while you drive (with Foveated DLSS on, at the next start), sharpening, ACC's own DLSS, and the optional
[Foveated DLSS](acc-foveated-dlss.md) (NVIDIA RTX, advanced setup). Cheeky's frame generation isn't available in ACC
yet.

## What's different with Sim-QVFR

- **Both eyes' sharp areas meet where you look** (convergence), on the dash as well as down the track, with or without eye
  tracking (Sim-QVFR uses how far away what you look at is).
- **Bloom and lens flares** look as they do without Sim-QVFR.
- **Contact shadows are off** while Sim-QVFR runs: those small shadows came out differently in the sharp area than
  around it.
